# Introduction
Introduction

This book is a guide for GearCats (FRC Team 6500) students and mentors. 
It explains how the team works, provides beginner-friendly tutorials, and points to helpful resources for learning more
It is open to continuous improvement from any member of the team.

## When you get stuck

- Do NOT be afraid - jump in, break things!
  - the goal is to have fun and learn!
  - we are a team, we are all learning, ask for help
- Ask a mentor or experienced student a specific question
  - on the programming team on chat
  - at a build meeting
- Bring the code, error, or robot behavior you are seeing.
